## Premise

_The Mountain in the Sea_ is a near-future sci-fi novel. In it, marine biologist
Dr. Ha Nguyen works with humanity's only sentient (this is hotly debated in the
book TODO: <-- make this sound... better?) android Evrim, and a Mongolian
mercenary drone operator named Altantsetseg travel to the Con Dao Archipelago
off the coast of Vietnam to investigate they believe to be a civilization of
intelligent octopuses capable of tool use, culture, and symbolic communication.

Some chapters focus on two other characters whose plots loosely entertwine with
the main story: Rustem, a Tatar hacker contracted to find an exploit in Evrim's
brain, and Eiko, a young Japanese man abducted in Vietnam and forced to work
onboard an AI-operated fishing vessel.

## Cool Things That Actually Exist

I always enjoy when books mention interesting, unknown-to-me facts about the
real world. In this case, Dr. Nguyen and Evrim are discussing the octopus
settlement they're studying and they compare it with
[Octopolis and Octlantis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Octopolis_and_Octlantis),
which I was surprised to learn are real locations off the coast of Australia
where gloomy octopuses are known to gather and reside. Wikipedia even calls them
"non-human settlements". So cool!
