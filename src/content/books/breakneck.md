## The Gist

are excellent at navigating legal quagmires and blocking anything, good or bad,
from getting done -- whereas China is a society run by engineers -- who
excellent at building public infrastructure and developing impressive technical
excellence in manufacturing, but almost to a fault. Like they build
compulsively.
