## Has this ever happened to you?

Since the end of college, I've been a one pencil / one pen kinda guy. Recently--
and by recently I mean like... 6 months ago-- I tossed my $25 rOtring 600
mechanical pencil in my bag, and when I removed it I was shocked to see that the
metal tube at the tip of the pencil was _bent_.

And it was at this moment that I decided to do something about this. I'd
recently joined a local makerspace, and I was looking for a project to do to
familiarize myself with their CNC wood router, so I figured I'd try and make a
nice case for the two writing utensils I use most regularly -- my rOtring
mechanical pencil and rOtring pen.

## Design

The design for this was pretty straightforward-- I iterated a bit on the initial
design via 3D printing and made small refinements here and there, but for the
most part the general shape remained the same across interations.

The design I settled on is a two part case, with magnets to hold the two pieces
together and a lip to align them, shown below.
